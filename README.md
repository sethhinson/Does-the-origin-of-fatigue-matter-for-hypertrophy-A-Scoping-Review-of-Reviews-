# Does the Origin of Fatigue Matter for Muscle Hypertrophy? A Scoping Review of Reviews

**Author(s):** Seth Hinson; Scott Mikula; Kristen Dunsmore; Zac Robinson; Micheal Hall; Eric Helms; Joseph Carzoli; Michael C. Zourdos
**Affiliation:** Muscle Physiology Laboratory, Florida Atlantic University
**Reporting guideline:** PRISMA Extension for Scoping Reviews (PRISMA-ScR; Tricco et al., 2018)
**Status:** Pre-data-collection registration

## About this repository

This repository is the public, version-controlled preregistration record for a scoping review of reviews examining the relationship between resistance training fatigue and muscle hypertrophy by mapping the available reviews comparing muscle hypertrophy outcomes between training protocols that increase acute training fatigue and traditional training protocols. It exists to make the registered protocol, and any subsequent amendments, independently verifiable via commit history.

- **`preregistration_scoping_review.md`** is the source of truth for the protocol. All edits are made here first.
- **`Preregistration_Scoping_Review.docx`** is a generated export for readability/sharing and should not be hand-edited; regenerate it from the Markdown source after any change.
- Protocol amendments made after registration are logged as dated, individual commits to `preregistration_scoping_review.md`, each with a brief rationale in the commit message, consistent with the *Protocol amendments* declaration in the preregistration itself.
